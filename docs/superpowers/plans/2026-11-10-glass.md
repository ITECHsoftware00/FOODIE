# Foodie glass design and implementation

Goal: adapt the supplied Facet reference to Foodie's existing discovery and demo ordering platform.

Design: charcoal smoked glass, silver bevels, restrained green accents, large white typography and food photography. Five irregular extruded glass panels form the hero. Pointer tilt and spring lift reveal the material. A reversible shatter transition opens a Foodie explanation in place. The existing catalogue and dialogs receive matching glass surfaces.

Approach: retain React/Vinext and local assets; dynamically load Three.js 0.169 for real lighting and geometry. CSS handles ordinary control feedback. A CSS glass composition and semantic DOM content remain available without WebGL. The supplied studio copy and no-images constraint belong to Facet, so Foodie's branding and imagery take precedence here.

Constraints: preserve ordering logic, keyboard controls, local persistence and demo boundaries. Reduced motion and pause disable movement; coarse pointers do not tilt. Cap DPR, reuse geometry, stop RAF offscreen, and dispose resources on unmount.
